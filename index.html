Platform overview: features, roles and security · October 2026 · Presented by Maryna Struts

What it is

A private company network, not a public social app

One place

Company news, branch updates, colleagues and direct messages in a single app.

Invitation only

Nobody can sign up on their own. Every account starts from an invitation by a manager, HR or admin.

Built for many branches

Regions and branches mirror CTDI’s structure. Each person sees what is relevant to them.

Runs in any browser today. The same code base is ready for Android, iOS and Windows apps.

Features

Everything an employee needs day to day

Feed

Posts with photos, video or PDF; likes, comments, reporting.

Company news

Official announcements, policies and events for the company, a region or a branch.

Messages

Private one-to-one chats with replies, edits and read status.

Directory and branches

Find colleagues by name or branch; each branch has its own page and feed.

Documents

PDFs open in a built-in viewer; download is a separate button.

Alerts

Notifications for likes, comments and news addressed to you.

Moderation

Managers review reported posts and remove or dismiss them.

Admin panel

Roles and users, regions and branches, invitations, audit log.

Security centre

Two-factor sign-in and Windows Hello / fingerprint for managers.

Organisation

The app mirrors CTDI’s structure

Company

CTDI as a whole

Regions

e.g. Canada

Branches

Mississauga, Meadowview, Oakville

People

Each belongs to one branch

Admins create, rename and remove regions and branches directly in the app (Admin → Structure).

Safeguards: a branch with employees, or a region with branches, cannot be deleted. Every change is logged.

Roles

Eight roles, each limited to its own scope

RoleWhat they can do
GuestRead only: feed, news, directory
EmployeePost, comment, like, send messages, report posts
Team leadSame as employee; shown as a team lead in the directory
HRInvite and archive staff in own branch; news for own branch or region
Branch managerModerate, invite, archive and publish news in own branch
Regional managerThe same for every branch in the region, plus region-wide news
Global adminWhole company: roles, structure, company-wide news, audit log
Super adminEverything above, plus appoints global admins and deletes accounts

Rule: nobody can grant a role equal to or higher than their own. HR and managers invite only guests, employees and team leads.

Company news

The right news reaches the right people

Global

Everyone in the company

Published by global admins and the super admin.

Region

All branches in one region

Published by regional managers and HR of that region.

Branch

One branch only

Published by its branch manager, HR, or the regional manager.

Each item is tagged Announcement, Policy or Event. Readers in the audience get a notification; nobody outside it can see the item.

Joining and leaving

Access is granted and removed by managers

New employee

  1. Manager, HR or admin creates an invitation for a work email
  2. Employee registers with that email and confirms it
  3. Role and branch are applied automatically from the invitation

Invitations expire after 14 days.

Employee leaves

  1. Manager or HR archives the profile
  2. Access stops immediately, including open sessions
  3. Super admin then deletes the account for good, or restores it

Every step is recorded in the audit log.

Built-in limits stop spam and abuse

Files

Photos, video up to 50 MB, PDF up to 20 MB. Word, Excel and other file types are blocked.

Reporting

Any employee can report a post. The branch or regional manager removes it or dismisses the report.

Passwords

At least 12 characters. Managers and admins also use two-factor sign-in.

Security

Several independent layers of protection

Closed entry

Invitation-only sign-up; email must be confirmed.

Two-factor for power

Admin and manager actions work only after a code or fingerprint check.

Rules in the database

Every table denies access by default. Tricking the app cannot bypass it.

Private files

Links work for 2 hours and only for people allowed to see the post.

Attack resistance

Protection against injection, impersonation and request flooding.

Audit trail

Admin actions are logged; the log cannot be edited or erased.

Error messages never reveal internal details or whether an email is registered.

Tested before launch

235 of 235

simulated attacks were blocked, while normal actions kept working.

Covered: giving yourself admin rights, reading other people’s chats and files, skipping two-factor, forging the author of a post, injection, and spam.

Data and privacy

Employee data stays in Canada and stays minimal

Canadian hosting

Database and files are stored in the Canada (Central) region.

No hidden location data

GPS and camera details are removed from every photo before upload.

Need-to-know access

People see their branch, their region and company-wide content only.

Right to be removed

A departing employee’s account and content can be deleted completely.

A privacy policy under PIPEDA (Canada’s privacy law) will be published before launch.
